Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Bloom Editorial, an imprint of Libo, an independent publisher of workbooks and guided journals. Libo and Bloom Editorial are trade names of Victoria Lozano. She is responsible for your data and can be reached at bloom@liboprint.com.

What we collect and why

Our email list. If you ask for printable pages from one of our books, we collect your email address and, if you choose to give it, your first name. We use them to send you the pages you asked for, news about new books from the same author, and occasional invitations to read a new book before it is published. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give by submitting the form and confirming by email (double opt-in).

Visits. We count visits and clicks with Umami, which does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. Our host processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the website and keep it secure (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Fonts and icons are served from this website, not from third parties.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we never sell or rent your data.

Who processes data for us

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): stores the email list, sends our emails and serves the sign-up form.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): hosts this website.
  • Umami Software, Inc.: cookieless visit statistics.

Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, ask for a copy, and withdraw your consent at any time. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Write to bloom@liboprint.com to use these rights. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Our email list is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Reviews

We never ask for a review in exchange for anything, and receiving our emails never depends on leaving one.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell subscribers by email.
