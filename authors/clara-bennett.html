Grief & remembering Mom

Clara Bennett

Journals and keepsakes for daughters who lost their mother.

Grief that doesn't follow a timeline or a rulebook. Clara's journals are not about getting over it. They are about carrying it, honoring her, and keeping her close on the anniversaries and the ordinary days.

From Clara's journal

Reaching for the phone is not a mistake. It is what love does out of habit. Clara Bennett · 2 min read When You Reach for the Phone to Call HerGrief does not wait for anniversaries. Sometimes it arrives in a parking lot, with your hand already reaching for her number. A letter you never send is still a letter. She is still the one it is for.
